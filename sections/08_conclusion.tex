\section{Conclusion}

We presented UAV-TAlign, a scene-level UAV RGB-thermal alignment framework that turns strong
off-the-shelf multimodal pairwise matching into reliability-controlled scene-level decisions. The
PRCV evidence shows that pairwise availability is already strong on UAV-TAlign-1K, and that the
decisive step is to transform abundant frame-level matches into retained scene-level alignment
products. Across formal evaluation, protocol-consistency analysis, and cumulative module
ablations, the strongest supported gains come from robust scene consensus and QA-aware
verification.

Together with UAV-TAlign-1K and the accompanying label-free evaluation setup, UAV-TAlign provides a
practical and reproducible foundation for studying real UAV RGB-thermal scene-level alignment.
