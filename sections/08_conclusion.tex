\section{Conclusion}

We presented UAV-TAlign, a scene-level UAV RGB-thermal alignment framework that turns strong
off-the-shelf multimodal pairwise matching into reliable scene-level decisions. The PRCV evidence
shows that pairwise availability is already strong on UAV-TAlign-1K, while scene-level reliability
remains the central challenge under realistic UAV conditions. Across formal evaluation,
protocol-consistency analysis, and cumulative module ablations, the strongest supported gains come
from robust scene consensus and QA-aware verification rather than from matcher availability alone.

Together with UAV-TAlign-1K and the accompanying label-free evaluation setup, this formulation
offers a practical and reproducible path for studying real UAV RGB-thermal scene-level alignment.
